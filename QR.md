One more important suggestion: use a dynamic QR code
Instead of generating a QR code directly from your landing page URL using a static QR generator, consider using a permanent branded URL, for example:

https://shiahumsafar.com/download

This URL should redirect to your landing page.

Benefits:
- You can change the destination later without reprinting QR cards.
- You can track QR campaign traffic.
- You can identify which marriage hall or campaign brings users.
- You can eventually route Android and iOS users appropriately.
You can also use separate campaign URLs:
- /download?source=marriage_hall
- /download?source=instagram
- /download?source=referral
